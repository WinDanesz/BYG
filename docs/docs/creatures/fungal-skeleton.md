# Fungal Skeleton

<BiomeScreenshot id="fungal_skeleton" name="Fungal Skeleton" />

*A bleached skeleton with mushrooms sprouting from its skull, and a nasty habit of spitting them at you.*

A hostile skeleton variant that never carries a bow. It only appears if Fungal Skeleton content (`fungalSkeletonContentEnabled`) is enabled. See [Creature Settings](../getting-started/configuration.md#creature-settings) and the [Content Settings](../getting-started/configuration.md#content-settings) to adjust its spawn weight or disable it.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Spawn biome | Fungal Jungle |
| Spawn weight | 20 (`fungalSkeletonSpawnWeight`) |
| Health | 24 |
| Movement speed | 0.25 |
| Ranged attack | Mushroom blob, 3 damage |
| Attack range | 12 blocks |
| Drops | Bones as usual; rarely a Purple Glowshroom |

- **Spits a blob of mushroom from its mouth** with a telegraphed attack instead of shooting arrows. It closes in until you are within range and in sight, then stands still and fires roughly every one and a half to three seconds, more often the closer you are.
