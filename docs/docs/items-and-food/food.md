# Food

Hunger and saturation values are the defaults from the [Food Settings](../getting-started/configuration.md#food-settings). Hunger is counted in hunger points (2 points is one drumstick). Most food content can be turned off with `foodContentEnabled` or the toggle of the plant it belongs to.

## Berry bushes and rudo

- **Blueberry and strawberry bushes** grow through stages 0, 1 and 2. A bush at stage 2 is ripe: right-click it to pick the berries. Picking gives **2 berries** by default and sets the bush back to stage 1, so it can be picked again.
- Each random tick a bush has a **50% chance** to advance a stage. **Bone meal** advances it with a **40% chance** per use and always uses up the bone meal.
- You can **plant a blueberry** by right-clicking grass with the berry. Strawberries and rudo beans work like seeds and plant on **farmland**.
- **Rudo stalks** grow through stages 0 to 4. Stage 0 advances on every random tick and later stages have a 50% chance. A fully grown stalk (stage 4) gives **3 beans** when you right-click it and goes back to stage 3. Breaking a fully grown stalk also gives 3 beans, and breaking a brand-new one (stage 0) gives 1 bean back.
- The berry count, growth chances and planting rules are set in the [Block Settings](../getting-started/configuration.md#block-settings) and [Item Settings](../getting-started/configuration.md#item-settings).

## Baobab fruit

<ItemIcon id="baobab_fruit" name="Baobab Fruit" size={96} />

Baobab fruit hangs **below Baobab leaves** and grows through five stages (0 to 4). A fruit grows on a timer (every 1800 ticks by default, with a 30% chance per timer) and can be sped up with bone meal (40% per use).

- **Right-click** a fruit at stage 2 or higher to harvest it. A ripe fruit (stage 4) gives 1 fruit and drops back one stage, so it can be harvested again.
- **Breaking** a ripe fruit drops 3 fruit. Half-grown fruit drops less.
- If the leaf above the fruit is removed, the fruit falls and drops its fruit.
- You can **plant a fruit yourself** by right-clicking the underside of a Baobab leaf with a baobab fruit.
- Eating a fruit **clears all your active potion effects**, and it can be eaten even when you are not hungry. Both are configurable.
- One baobab fruit crafts into one **Baobab Powder**, used to brew the [Potion of Clarity](../brewing.md).

## Berry juice

Berry Juice is a drink (16 per stack). Drinking it restores 2 hunger and, on top of that, **heals you by 4 health points (2 hearts)** and gives **Regeneration for 5 seconds**. You get the empty glass bottle back. The heal and the regeneration time are configurable.

## Maple syrup

Maple Sap is collected from a maple tap and is smelted into Maple Syrup. A bottle of syrup is **not eaten in one go**: it has **3 sips** (configurable). Each sip restores 3 hunger and gives **Speed I for 40 seconds**, and there is a one-second pause between sips. After the last sip you keep an empty glass bottle. Syrup can be eaten even when you are not hungry.

## Golden beetroot

<ItemIcon id="golden_beetroot" name="Golden Beetroot" size={96} />

- **Eaten by you**: restores 6 hunger and gives **Regeneration I for 2 minutes** (configurable).
- **Fed to a tamed animal** (a tamed wolf, cat, parrot, horse and so on): the animal gains **permanent slow regeneration**. It heals half a heart every 100 ticks while it is hurt. The effect is saved with the animal. Right-click the animal with the beetroot; you do not eat it. Each animal can only be given it once.
